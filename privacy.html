Privacy policy

Drive Backup is a personal backup tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

The owner's own Google Drive, to store and update backup copies of the owner's files.

What it collects

Nothing. It does not collect, store, share or sell any data about anyone.

Where data goes

Backup files go only into the owner's own Google Drive. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

This website

These pages use no cookies, analytics, scripts or tracking.
