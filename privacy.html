Privacy policy

Drive Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner. It backs up the owner's files and keeps the owner's calendar and tasks in sync with the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

The owner's own Google Drive, to store and update backup copies of the owner's files.

The owner's own Google Calendar and Google Tasks, to keep them in sync with the owner's own computer.

What it collects

Nothing. It does not collect, store, share or sell any data about anyone.

Where data goes

Backup files go only into the owner's own Google Drive. Calendar and task data moves only between the owner's own Google account and the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

This website

These pages use no cookies, analytics, scripts or tracking.
